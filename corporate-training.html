How our engagement works

  1. 01Initial discussion to understand team profiles, operational context and training goals.
  2. 02Training-needs assessment or skills-gap review, where required.
  3. 03Programme outline with topics, learning outcomes, participant numbers and delivery mode.
  4. 04Agreement on assessment, feedback, documentation and any lawful certification requirements.
  5. 05Training delivery and post-programme review, subject to signed scope and availability.
