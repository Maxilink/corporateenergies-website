About CorporateEnergies

A learning partner for the energy industry, in Nigeria and beyond.

Who we are

Linking industry requirements to the competencies that meet them

CORPORATEENERGIES LIMITED is a Nigerian-registered company focused on professional, vocational, technical and executive education, capacity development and associated learning services for the energy industry.

We bring together technical capability, workplace safety, managerial effectiveness, workforce assurance and the tools that support continuous learning.

Our Services
Purpose, mission & vision

What drives us

Our Purpose

To contribute to a knowledgeable, competent and safety-conscious energy workforce through purposeful education, skills development and institutional learning solutions.

Our Mission

To support energy professionals and organisations with practical, relevant and accessible training, competence-development and learning solutions that strengthen workforce capability and professional growth.

Our Vision

To become a trusted partner in energy-sector learning, competence and professional development in Nigeria and beyond.
